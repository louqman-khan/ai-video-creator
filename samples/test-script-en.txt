Early morning light spreads over a quiet lake in the mountains. The water is so still that the snowy peaks appear twice, once in the sky and once below.
Further down the valley, a small village wakes up. Smoke rises from the chimneys, and farmers lead their cows out to the green fields.
In the afternoon, the weather changes. Dark clouds roll in, and heavy rain falls on the old stone bridge and the river beneath it.
By evening the storm has passed. Families gather around a table to share a warm meal, and children play outside under a clear, golden sky.
When night comes, the whole valley is silent. Millions of stars shine above the mountains, and the lake becomes a mirror for the sky.
