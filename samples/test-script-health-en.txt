Good health starts with small habits that you repeat every day. You do not need expensive equipment or a complicated plan to begin.
Start your morning with a glass of water and a few minutes of gentle stretching. Your body has been still all night, and it will thank you for waking up slowly.
Fill your plate with colourful vegetables, fresh fruit, and whole grains. Try to eat at regular times, and drink water through the day instead of waiting until you feel thirsty.
Walk for at least thirty minutes whenever you can. Take the stairs, walk to the market, or play with your children in the park. Moving a little every day protects your heart and lifts your mood.
Sleep matters just as much as food and exercise. Switch off your phone an hour before bed, keep your room dark and quiet, and aim for seven to eight hours of rest.
Remember that your mind needs care too. Spend time with people you love, take a few deep breaths when you feel stressed, and do not be afraid to ask for help.
If something feels wrong, see a doctor early. Regular check-ups can find problems while they are still small and easy to treat. Take care of your health today, and it will take care of you for years.
